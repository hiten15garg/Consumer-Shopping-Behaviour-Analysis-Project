
# Consumer Shopping Behaviour Analysis Project
## Project Overview
This project consists of Exploratory data analysis (EDA) on the Consumer shopping dataset
using python.

It involves major points:
1. Conumer spending analysis.
2. Performance in sales across product category,  country, age group, weather and so on.
3. Visualizing data with charts using matplotlib and seaborn.

The purpose of this analysis is get insights related to costumer preferences and using it to improve sales Performance by making best strategies and data driven decision making.

## Tools & Libraries Used
1. Python (Pandas, NumPy)
2. Seaborn (countplot, pie charts)
3. Matplotlib
4. Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset
1. Customer ID
1. Age
1. Gender
1. Item Purchased
1. Category
1. Purchase Amount (USD)
1. Location
1. Size
1. Color
1. Season
1. Review Rating
1. Subscription Status
1. Shipping Type
1. Discount Applied
1. Promo Code Used
1. Previous Purchases
1. Payment Method
1. Frequency of Purchases

## Project Tasks
1. Data Cleaning: Handling missing values and formatting data types.
2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):
Distribution of Sales by gender, age group, Category, products, Season and so on.

Analysis of average purchase amounts.

Analysis of average Reviews across product or Category.

Identification of top-selling product categories.

Analysis of Different age group spending Behaviour.

3. Visualizing:

Pie chart and bar chart for Sales across Category

Heatmap for average Review rating across category and product

Boxplot for Review rating and purchase amounts

Barchar for Distribution of sales among Different
Age group


## Key Finding
1. Clothing category Genrated more sale with (44.7%) and 1737 products

2. Age group with 48 - 55 purchase more with sales of $37282

3. In 14 and 90 day span, Most customers purchase clothing and accessories categories
